Hi!


Thanks for checking out my site! I'm a passionate photographer and videographer in San Diego, California. My camera allows me to capture all of life's adventures.

With a vast array of hobbies, I strive to recreate the experiences of the moment through my lens. Beyond the camera, I'm an avid explorer with a love for the outdoors. When I'm not behind the lens you can find me skiing in the mountains, cycling along the coast, or surfing at one of the many breaks in southern California. With all these activities I end up bringing my camera anyways so I can relive the feelings of adrenaline and beauty. Plus it's always good practice.

I'd love the opportunity to create. Whether it's lifestyle, adventure, products, events, or something new, I'm fully on board :)
